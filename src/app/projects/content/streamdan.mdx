StreamDan is the customer-facing website for an IPTV subscription and service offering, implemented as a custom WordPress theme in PHP.

## Project overview

The site presents the service, plans, supported devices, frequently asked questions, and supporting content through a responsive structure designed to help visitors move between service information and contact options.

## Custom WordPress implementation

The implementation uses WordPress with a purpose-built PHP theme rather than Elementor or a purchased theme. That approach keeps the presentation aligned with the project's content needs while retaining WordPress publishing and administration workflows.

## UX and content structure

The homepage establishes the service proposition and directs visitors toward plans and device information. Supporting navigation organizes plans, devices, blog content, FAQs, company information, and contact paths into a straightforward customer-facing experience that adapts across screen sizes.

## SEO considerations

Technical SEO considerations were incorporated into the ongoing site work. Search Console and indexing were part of that process, and the WordPress content structure supports SEO-oriented publishing and blog strategy. No traffic, ranking, conversion, or performance outcome is claimed here.
